Why shouldn't we trust atoms?

They make up everything. Even this answer! Atoms are the building blocks of matter — so trusting them means trusting literally everything around you. A playful paradox.

What do you call someone with no body and no nose?

Nobody knows. The classic play on words: "nobody" means no person, and "knows" sounds like "nose". Pure comedic gold.

What's the object-oriented way to become wealthy?

Inheritance. Because in programming (and in life), inheriting properties from a parent class — or a rich relative — is a shortcut to wealth.

How many tickles does it take to tickle an octopus?

Ten-tickles! Because octopuses have eight arms and two tentacles — a playful math joke that multiplies the giggles.

What is: 1 + 1?

Depends on who you're asking. Mathematician: 2. Philosopher: a window. Romantic: us. Developer: 11 (string concatenation). Context is everything.
